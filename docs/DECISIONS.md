- **Bug found in the unit tests:** the Week 10 policy reader took "Los artículos en promoción no aceptan devoluciones" to mean the store accepts no returns at all, so it marked full-price items final sale too. **Fix:** a no-returns sentence only applies store-wide when it doesn't mention discounts.

## Week 13 · Session 2 (2026-11-08) — real-user fix #1: long carts
- **Evidence (two real sessions, phone):** Session 1 (Amazon): *"no me dejó subir múltiples fotos entonces no enfoqué todo el carrito."* The event log shows the same visit uploading twice, then choosing "I'll wait." Session 2 (Zara app, 24): the result read three items for **MX$4,997 at risk** while the screen's total was **MX$9,492**. The missing items weren't counted at all, so the app showed less risk than the buyer actually had (breaks Team 8 Condition 4, no false certainty). The same session showed "tienda desconocida": the Zara app never prints the store name on the cart, so the Zara rule in our table never fired.
- **Pain ranking (by threat to the 10:1 case):** 1) long carts: missing items understate the risk (2 of 2 users); 2) unknown store / missing rule: "everything at risk" with no useful information (2 of 2: *"lo usaría si me diera más información"*); 3) language: user 2 ran Safari's auto-translate, which mistranslates "size" as "tamaño" and garbles other lines.
- **Fix #1 shipped:** up to 4 screenshots of one cart are read and merged into a single check (`lib/merge.ts`); a line that shows up in two screenshots counts once, repeats inside one screenshot are kept. "+ Add another screenshot of this cart" appears under every real result. When the items read (plus shipping) don't add up to the total on the screen, the gap is shown as "Not read from your screenshot yet" and a high flag tells the buyer to add the rest of the cart.
- **Fix #2 (cheap, same deploy):** when no store name is visible, a high flag asks the buyer to type it under "What we read" (e.g. Zara), which turns the table rule on.
- 18/18 tests pass (new: session-2 numbers flagged, no false alarm when totals match, two-screenshot merge, real duplicates kept).
- Language (pain #3) stays logged for next week: the fix is a real ES version, not machine translation.
- **Live test after deploy (fictional two-screenshot cart):** before the fix, one screenshot showed MX$4,997 at risk against a MX$9,492 total, with no warning (same as session 2). After the fix, the first screenshot showed "Not read from your screenshot yet: MX$4,495" plus "Add a screenshot of the rest of your cart"; adding the second screenshot found the store (ZARA), applied the Zara rule and left nothing unread. **Bug found in that test:** the reader returned size "M" on one screenshot and "Talla M" on the other, so the overlapping dress was counted twice ("appears twice"). Fixed by normalizing names and sizes before comparing; regression test added (19/19).
- **Persona test (Layer 1, Mariana, 24, Zara app):** worst confusion was the big red number reading as the final answer while part of the cart was unread. Fixed: an amber note under the headline whenever something is unread. Logged: the store-name field is far below its prompt; Spanish UI next week. ## Week 14 · Session 1 (2026-11-13) — Pricing: packet, build, hosting move
- **Packet before code** (`docs/PACKET.md`, commit 661434e): segments, success definition, image-generated mockup, Mermaid flowchart + swimlane + simulator flow, scope cut, test plan T1–T13. Week 13 packet archived in `docs/w13/`.
- **One source of truth:** `lib/pricing.ts` holds plans, prices, fees and every assumption with a measured / sourced / assumed tag. `/pricing`, `/simulator` and the `/about` 10:1 memo all import it; a test fails if one of them hard-codes a number (T12).
- **Prices (from my Brain Bending brief and the Team 5 Blueprint):** Free = 1 full check a month per device · 4-check pack MX$60 (MX$15 a check) · 30-day pass MX$49, no auto-renew. Issuer = second payer, shown as a bet, not sold. Return-window alerts are **not sold** because they aren't built (Blueprint Condition 6).
- **Real money, no processor.** I don't have a Mercado Pago account, and the pilot is five buyers: they pay by bank transfer or cash, person to person, never on the site. Transfer costs MX$0, so the simulator's payment method defaults to transfer; Mercado Pago's fee (3.49% + MX$4 + IVA) stays as the "at scale" option.
- **No founder contact on the page.** Buyers ask through "Request the pack": Google sign-in, optional WhatsApp, explicit consent, stored in `byp_pack_requests` (RLS: own rows; founder reads in the dashboard; buyer can delete). Requests count in the ledger's "asked for the pack" column.
- **Codes:** 10 pack codes + 5 pass codes generated offline; only their SHA-256 hashes are in Supabase (`byp_codes`, RLS on, no policies). The plain codes and the hashes are never committed. One RPC (`byp_use_check`, security definer) checks or uses a code; a pack check is used only after a screenshot is actually read.
- **Shadow clause in code:** `analyze()` takes only the cart and the rule. Test T5 asserts arity 2, deep-equal results in a free and a paid context, and that the checker calls `analyze(ex, rule)` and nothing else.
- **Known limit:** the free counter lives in the browser (clearing it resets it) and `/api/extract` isn't metered server-side. Fine for a five-buyer pilot; not for scale.
