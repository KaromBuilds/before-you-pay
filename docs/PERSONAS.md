# PERSONAS — Before You Pay

*Two formal customer personas built from ten weeks of User-role research, mainly my Week 10 Technologist brief and my family's real buying pattern. Names and details are invented. These are the people my persona tests impersonate and my marketing targets. Version 1 (Week 12), to be corrected when real users say otherwise.*

---

## Persona 1 — Rocío Hernández

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 51 |
| **Context** | Lives in Chalco, Estado de México. Runs the household and buys clothes online for herself and her two kids on her phone, mostly from Zara and department-store apps, because the nearest mall is an hour away. Pays with a debit card or the card her son set up for her. Reads on a phone with big font. Uses WhatsApp all day and distrusts new apps. |
| **The need, in her words** | *"I never know if they'll take it back. The size is always a guess, and when it doesn't fit I end up keeping it because the return is a hassle or they tell me it was on sale."* |
| **What she does today** | Guesses the size from what she bought last time, adds two sizes "just in case" when there's a discount, and pays. She has never opened a store's return policy page. When something doesn't fit, she gives it away or it stays in the closet. |
| **What she'd pay** | Nothing per check. She would use it free and recommend it to her sister. *Hypothesis:* if anyone pays for her, it is not the store. |
| **What would make her quit** | Having to create an account before seeing anything; English-only legal text; a result she has to scroll to find; any hint that the app is selling her something. |

**Persona-test instruction:** *"You are Rocío, 51, from Chalco. You buy clothes for your family on your phone. You read slowly, you don't trust new apps, and you quit silently the moment you feel lost or think someone wants your data."*

## Persona 2 — Diego Ramírez

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 21 |
| **Context** | University student in Mexico City. Buys jackets, sneakers and jeans online, almost always with a welcome code, a student code or during Hot Sale / Buen Fin, often on *meses sin intereses*. Knows his size is L in some brands and M in others, but keeps that in his head. |
| **The need, in his words** | *"The discount is what makes me buy, and it's also what kills the return. I only find out after it arrives too big."* |
| **What he does today** | Fills the cart fast before the timer ends, sometimes buys the same item in two sizes, pays on installments. He learned the "discounted items are final sale" rule the hard way, after paying. |
| **What he'd pay** | *Hypothesis:* up to about MX$29 a month for saved history across many stores, but only if the single check stays free. Never for a version funded by stores. |
| **What would make him quit** | Slow answers (more than about 30 seconds), being told what to buy, generic advice that ignores the actual screenshot, or a result that says "safe" and turns out wrong. |

**Persona-test instruction:** *"You are Diego, 21, a student in CDMX. You're about to pay for two discounted jackets with a 20% code and a 'Only 3 left' banner. You're in a hurry, you skim, and you abandon anything that takes more than half a minute."*
